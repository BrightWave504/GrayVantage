Certified Professional Office Administrator (CPOA)

Learn First, Excel Later — A Learning-First Approach Before Assessment

CPOA is a private competency-based micro-credential recognizing competence in office management, administrative support, and organizational operations.

The program develops communication, records management, customer relations, professional ethics, and administrative skills.

The CPOA program is open to all courses and disciplines because office administration and workplace skills are relevant across industries.

Certified Junior Marketing Associate (CJMA)

Learn First, Excel Later — A Learning-First Approach Before Assessment

The Certified Junior Marketing Associate is a private competency-based certification program for students and professionals seeking to validate their modern marketing competencies.

Participants develop practical expertise in branding, digital marketing, consumer behavior, market research, marketing communications, campaign execution, and data-driven decision-making.
