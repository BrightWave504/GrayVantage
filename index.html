Certification and Data Privacy Disclaimer

The CPOA and CJMA certification programs are conducted by GrayVantage Academic & Corporate Services for educational and competency development purposes.

These are private competency-based certifications and are not equivalent to government-issued professional licenses, Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) accreditation, or professional regulatory certifications.

Certificate issuance is subject to completion of the applicable training requirements and successful passing of the assessment.

Personal information collected through the application process will be used for registration, communication, identity verification, examination administration, and certification purposes in accordance with applicable Philippine data privacy laws.

Personal information will be handled securely and will not be disclosed to unauthorized third parties, except where consent is provided or disclosure is required by law.
