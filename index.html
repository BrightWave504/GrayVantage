CPOA: Learn First, Excel Later

A Learning-First Approach Before Assessment.

CPOA is a micro-credential that recognizes individuals with advanced competence in office management, administrative support, and organizational operations.

The program equips participants with essential skills in communication, records management, customer relations, and professional ethics.

The CPOA Program is open to all courses and disciplines since effective office administration, communication, records management, coordination, and professional workplace skills are essential across all industries and organizations.

Certified Junior Marketing Associate (CJMA)

GrayVantage Academic & Corporate Services

DTI Registration No. 7316721 | BIR Registration No. 487617982

In partnership with Reccsar Private Limited, Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India.

CJMA: Learn First, Excel Later

A Learning-First Approach Before Assessment.

The Certified Junior Marketing Associate is a private competency-based certification program designed for students and professionals seeking to strengthen and validate their competencies in modern marketing practice.

The program develops practical expertise in branding, digital marketing, consumer behavior, market research, and marketing communication strategies.

Participants develop the ability to create impactful campaigns, understand target markets, and apply data-driven marketing decisions.
