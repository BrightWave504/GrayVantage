Professional Certification Program

Certified Professional Office Administrator (CPOA)

CPOA: Learn First, Excel Later – A Learning-First Approach Before Assessment.

CPOA is a micro-credential recognizing competencies in office management, administrative support, and organizational operations.

The program develops communication, records management, customer relations, coordination, and professional ethics.

The CPOA program welcomes participants from all courses and disciplines because administrative and workplace competencies are applicable across industries.

Who Should Attend?

  • Administrative assistants and office managers
  • BSOA students and students from other programs
  • Aspiring and working professionals
